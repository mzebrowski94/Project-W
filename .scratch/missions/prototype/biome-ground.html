How a biome's ground is laid

The question (ticket 02). Which technique lays one biome's ground from a seed - a base ground, patches of a neighbouring ground, rock of a given density - so it reads like the hand-painted Adventure biomes and can never seal its own gaps? And therefore: which ground fields does a biome definition need?

Each row starts with the Adventure's own biome (black border), then six seeds of the generator. Switch the technique for patches and for rock independently; edit the definitions at the bottom and everything re-lays. Walls, gaps, aprons and the connectivity check are the same in every technique.

gap apron (base ground only) one-tile slot (narrower than a brute) pocket - ground you cannot reach

A whole strip

The two Adventures as they are, then a generated mission strip: four biomes dealt from the pool (none twice, never the same base side by side) and a bare finale stand-in. Patches marked neighbour take the base ground of the biome on the nearer side, which is what the Adventure's own comment says it does by hand.

The definitions

The ground keys as the ticket 03 grilling settled them (roster and furniture are not drawn here). Every range is drawn from the mission seed. Edits re-lay everything.

What each field does

widthMin/Max - columns between its walls. regions - shading cells in the biome.
ground.base - the ground the biome is painted in first.
ground.patches - layers painted in order, each shape ground density sizeMin sizeMax: shape rect / blob (ragged is here only to compare), ground a terrain or neighbour - the base of the biome on the nearer side, or ground.fallback when there is none or it is this biome's own. Patches go down until their density of the biome is covered, each size tiles a side.
ground.outcrops - bars length long and thickness thick, an arm bent off one end at a share arms of them, until their density of the biome is rock.
ground.crags - smoothed noise thresholded at its density; smoothing passes make blobs out of speckle. Laid before outcrops; repaired - doors opened, narrow passages widened, pockets sealed.

sim.mission.ground.* - the same for every biome. apron (tiles in front of a pass that stay base ground), clearance (no passage narrower than this many tiles, anywhere - 2 is ten world units, a brute is 5.2 across).
