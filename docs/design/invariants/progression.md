# Invariants: Progression

Part of the invariants indexed in [`../README.md`](../README.md#invariants), which says what a
status means. If a change would break a row here, stop and raise it rather than deciding alone.

| Invariant | Status |
|---|---|
| Nothing is bought, equipped or configured between levels. | judgement |
| **The proving ground is practice** (G0060): its setup - gun, squad, ranks - reaches its own world and nothing else, and it earns no draft, the boss included. | enforced - `ProvingGroundTest.earnsNoDraftForKillingTheBoss`, `.holdsTheChosenRanksFromTheFirstTick`; that the campaign never reads a setup is judgement |
| **A tally belongs to a life** (G0044): what a commander has killed, taken and stabilised lives on him and dies with him; the man who respawns starts from nothing, and no code anywhere resets a tally. | enforced - `TallyTest.startsFromNothingWhenHeComesBack` |
| **A kill is credited to the slot the killing blow carried**, stamped where the blow was spawned - his shot, his sweep, his grenade, the gun he crews - and read in one place, the death. Never by entity, never by whoever the grid visited first: two players' blows on one tick credit the higher slot, the rule orders follow (G0028). | enforced - `TallyTest.creditsTheKillToTheSlotWhoseShotLandedIt`, `.creditsTheKillToWhoeverThrewTheGrenade`, `.creditsTheKillToTheCommanderCrewingTheGun`, `.creditsTheHigherSlotWhenTwoPlayersLandBlowsOnOneTick` |
| **A blast is credited to whoever's blow set it off** - the thing that explodes carries the credit of the blow that killed it, so a barrel or a charged body counts for the man who shot it. A blast nobody caused, and a trooper's kill, count for nobody. | enforced - `TallyTest.creditsABlastToWhoeverSetItOff`, `.creditsNothingForAKillNoPlayerCaused` |
| **Only the other side's dead are kills.** Friendly fire is neither counted nor subtracted. | enforced - `TallyTest.countsNothingForKillingSomebodyOnHisOwnSide` |
| **A goal is a control point taken while he stood inside it**; a stabilise is any man he put back on his feet, another player's commander included. | enforced - `TallyTest.countsAPointTakenWhileHeStoodOnIt`, `.countsAStabiliseForTheManWhoFinishedIt`, `.countsPuttingAnotherPlayerBackOnHisFeet` |
| **A tally is shown, never spent** (pillar 3): nothing buys, unlocks or scales with it. | judgement |
| **An upgrade changes numbers, never what an entity is made of** (ADR 0017). Behaviour an upgrade unlocks is carried from spawn by every unit that could use it and is off at zero. | enforced - `ArchitectureTest.onlyTheSpawnerChangesWhatAnEntityIsMadeOf`, `CompositionTest`; the effects are numbers by construction (`Effect`) |
| **Every number an upgrade may move has exactly one reader**, and a stat nobody reads fails the build. | enforced - `ArchitectureTest.everyStatIsReadInExactlyOnePlace` |
| **A unit spawned after an upgrade is identical to one that was on the field when it landed.** Numbers are folded where they are read, never written back. | enforced - `UpgradeTest.readsTheSameCooldownForATrooperOnTheFieldAndOneWhoArrivesAfterwards`, `.chargesAPullTheShorterWaitFromTheTickAfterThePick` |
| The same seed and the same inputs deal the same drafts; a pick is an input event. | enforced - `DraftTest.dealsTheSameDraftsAndAppliesTheSamePicksOnEveryRun`, `.carriesTheLedgerAndTheDraftsThroughASnapshot`, `DeterminismFuzzTest` (random picks and presence) |
| **A pick or a crate choice made on this machine reaches the world once, whenever it was made** - on a frame that runs no tick, while paused, or before an online session plays or its seat has a slot. It is held until a tick or the session takes it, and offered again until then. | enforced - `OnlineSessionTest.deliversAPickMadeBeforeTheSessionIsPlaying`, `ScenarioSessionTest.deliversAPickGivenOnAFrameTooShortToRunATick`, `.spendsAPickOnceEvenWhenAFrameRunsSeveralTicks`, `.remembersAPickMadeWhilePaused`, `HeldEventsTest` |
| **Every seated slot is dealt his own drafts, solo or in company** (G0118): no rotation, nobody's card waits on anybody else's, and a draft belongs to its chooser alone - a pick from anyone else, or for a draft that is gone, changes nothing (G0049). | enforced - `DraftTest.dealsEverySeatedSlotHisOwnDraftForASiteTaken`, `.changesNothingForAPickThatIsWrongInAnyWay`, `.changesNothingForAKindTheDraftDoesNotOffer` |
| **Nothing is passed on** (G0118): an away slot keeps his drafts and goes on being dealt them, and a slot seated mid-level is owed every threshold his count passes, the sites already taken included. | enforced - `DraftTest.keepsTheDraftsOfAnAwaySlotAndGoesOnDealingHim`, `.owesASlotSeatedLateTheDraftsOfEverySiteAlreadyTaken`, `CrossingTest.catchesUpEveryRidgeWestOfWhereHeComesOn`; online `SessionTest.keepsADroppedPlayersDraftsAndTakesHimBackWhenSomebodySitsInHisSlot` |
| **An upgrade belongs to the slot or the side, never to a life** - a commander who respawns keeps what his slot chose, the opposite of the tally. | enforced - `DraftTest.keepsHisSlotsPersonalRanksWhenHeComesBack` |
| **Drafts come from two sources and nothing else** (G0118): a site taken, for every seated slot, and a slot's own crossing - his commander into a ridge's pass, once a ridge, never waiting on the biome's sites. Hold waves and bosses earn nothing. | enforced - `DraftTest.earnsNothingWhileNothingIsTaken`, `CrossingTest.earnsHisSlotADraftWhenHisCommanderEntersARidge`, `.countsARidgeOnceHoweverOftenHeCrossesIt`, `.countsACrossingForHisOwnSlotAlone`, `.neverWaitsOnTheSitesOfTheBiomeHeLeaves`; that nothing else is counted is by construction - `UpgradeSystem` reads `Site.taken` and commanders' positions alone |
| **The schedule is fixed when the level is built** (G0118): every site and ridge weighed, `drafts x (sources / sourcesRef) ^ (curvePercent / 100)` planned through `StrictMath` and rounded once by `Whole.nearest`, the first at once and the rest one even gap apart, the same thresholds for every slot; a tier opens by share of a slot's own planned drafts. | enforced - `DraftScheduleTest`, `DraftTest.earnsOnTheScheduleTheLevelWasPlannedWith`, `.plansTheScheduleOnceFromWhatTheLevelWasBuiltWith`, `.dealsATierOnceItsShareOfHisPlannedDraftsIsBehindHim`, `CrossingTest.plansACrossingForEveryRidgeOfTheMap` |
| The relational guarantees `SimConfigTest` holds hold at every upgrade's and adaptation's highest rank, through the same fold the game uses. Body size is never a stat. | enforced - `SimConfigTest.runsHotterThanAnyOtherGunAManCanCarryWithEveryUpgradeAtItsHighestRank`, `.keepsTheSquadSlowerThanTheCommanderAtEveryUpgradesHighestRank`, `.letsGoOfHimSoonerThanAnythingElseOnTheFieldDoesWithEveryAdaptationAtItsHighestRank`, `.neverGivesUpInsideTheRangeItNoticesHimAtWithEveryAdaptationAtItsHighestRank` - every upgrade *and adaptation* held at its highest rank (`SimConfigTest.everythingHeld`) |
| Every upgrade and adaptation in force is on screen with its rank, and so is who holds a draft (pillar 5). A card's text is generated from its effects, so tuning a number cannot leave a card lying. | enforced - `ReadoutWriterTest.showsWhoHoldsADraftAndItsCardsWithTheRankEachWouldHave`, `.listsWhatIsHeldWithItsRanksAndTheMostItCanHave`, `CardTextTest.hasWordsForEveryShippedUpgradeThatFitACard`; the aside and the tell are judgement, `ReadoutWriterTest.marksWhatTheSwarmHoldsApartFromWhatTheSideHolds`, `UpgradeReporterTest.saysTheSwarmAdaptedAndNamesNoPlayer`; an adaptation is written in the swarm's colour (judgement), and an upgrade earned, passed on or taken, or an adaptation, is also typed over the view as a toast (`ToastTest`) |
| **The swarm adapts on the drafts' schedule only** (G0118) - planned from the level's sources when it is built, moved by its spread and never past `mostPercent` of the pool; its count the side's, the sites taken and the furthest biome any commander has entered, each worth `perPlayerPercent` more per player seated past the first and the share risen by as much, never past `ceilingPercent`; its first a gap in - dealt from a stream of its own, with nobody choosing (D11). Never by time or by cards taken. An adaptation narrowed to a strain moves that strain's numbers alone. | enforced - `AdaptationScheduleTest`, `AdaptationTest.adaptsOnceForEverySiteTheSideTakes`, `.adaptsWhenACommanderFirstEntersTheBiomeBeyondARidgeAndNotBefore`, `.countsEachBiomeOnceForTheWholeSide`, `.waitsAGapBeforeItsFirstAdaptationWhereADraftComesAtOnce`, `.makesASourceWorthMoreForEveryPlayerSeatedPastTheFirst`, `.holdsNoMoreThanItsShareOfThePoolAndNeverPastTheCeilingInCompany`, `.opensAnAdaptationsTierByShareOfTheSwarmsOwnPlan`; that nothing else is counted is by construction - `UpgradeSystem` reads `Site.taken` and commanders' positions alone |
| A machine's top bar shows at most one draft **or one crate** (G0056), and never over the view. Only the seat that opened it drives it. A second local seat's crate is not opened while the bar is taken, and one that opens anyway is shut. | enforced - `DraftBarTest.belongsToTheSeatThatOpenedIt`, `CrateBarTest.belongsToTheSeatWhoseCrateItIs`, `.leavesTheBarToADraftAndNamesTheCrateToShut`, `.namesASecondLocalSeatWithACrateOpenForShutting`, `TopBarSeatsTest.opensNoDraftWhileACrateHasTheBar`, `.keepsAnotherSeatFromOpeningACrateWhileTheBarIsTaken`, `.shutsASecondLocalSeatsCrateSoAMachineHoldsOne`; drawn in the bar only (`TopBarPainter`) |
| Opening a draft **or a crate** pauses the game only while one player is in it - offline or online, a player whose link dropped not counted - and closing resumes only that pause, as does a second player sitting down (D2). | enforced - `DraftPauseTest`, `SessionTest.tellsEveryMachineAPauseIsForADraft`, `SessionTest.liftsADraftPauseWhenASecondPlayerSitsDown` |
| While a draft is open its seat's order inputs work it and give no order; every other seat plays on (G0049). | enforced - `DraftKeysTest.takesTheChoosersOrderAndLeavesEveryOtherSeatsAlone` |
| A crewed turret fires the place's gun (G0024), and no upgrade covers it. | enforced - `UpgradeTest.reachesNoTurret` |
| **The swarm's health is one percentage** on every strain's and the nest's, applied where their definitions are bound - so a unit spawned, evolved or checked by a test sees the same number - and every relational guarantee in `SimConfigTest` holds on the scaled numbers. Barrels, mines, rabbits and turrets are not the swarm. | enforced - `SwarmHealthTest`, `SimConfigTest.deletesAnOrdinaryEnemyWithOneSwingAndNotABrute` on the scaled numbers; one reader - `SimConfig.healthOf`, `.nestHealth` |
| **A tier moves the swarm's numbers and nothing else** (G0063): count, health, nest period and how many adaptations it plans, read by `MissionConfig` alone and folded into the config before the world exists - never damage, never the player's side. Every relational guarantee in `SimConfigTest` holds at every tier. | enforced - `MissionConfigTest`; every relational guarantee in `SimConfigTest` runs off a mission and at each tier through `MissionConfig.apply`; one reader - `ArchitectureTest.onlyMissionConfigReadsATier`; both percents rounded once - `WholeTest.multipliesBothPercentsBeforeRoundingOnce` |
| **Looter adds at most one card to a crate, ever** (G0110): the first opening by a commander holding it, of a kind new to the crate, never past four - whoever opened it before, and whoever's Looter opens it after. | enforced - `CrateTest.holdsOneCardMoreOnALooterHoldersFirstOpening`, `.givesNothingMoreOnTheLooterHoldersSecondOpening`, `.givesNothingMoreOnAnotherLooterHoldersLaterOpening`, `.addsACardOfANewKindToACrateANonLooterOpenedFirst`, `.neverHoldsMoreThanACrateCanUnderLooter` |
