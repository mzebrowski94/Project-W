# Invariants: Wounds and casualties

Part of the invariants indexed in [`../README.md`](../README.md#invariants), which says what a
status means. If a change would break a row here, stop and raise it rather than deciding alone.

| Invariant | Status |
|---|---|
| **What a blow does to a man is declared by the source, never derived from the amount** (G0020, G0053): one of three harms - wound, down, kill. A man has no health, so an amount is never read for him at all. | enforced - `VitalityTest.woundsAManWithoutPuttingHimDownHoweverHardTheBlow`, `CasualtyTest.leavesATrooperShotDownOnTheGroundRatherThanDead`, `.killsATrooperOutrightWhenTheBlowIsOneThatKills` |
| **Gunfire wounds; blasts put a man down, except a grenade's, which wounds; claws, a sweep, a leap and an ant's last bite kill** (G0053). That asymmetry is what makes distance worth keeping. Declared where each blow is spawned, by category - `Spawner` - except the blast kinds, which differ and so are keys (`sim.blast.harm`, `sim.grenade.blastHarm`). | enforced - `CombatTest.letsAnAlienProjectileHitTheCommander`, `.killsTheCommanderTheMomentAnEnemyTouchesIt`, `BlastTest.catchesTheCommanderAsReadilyAsAnEnemy`, `GrenadeTest.woundsAManCaughtInItsBlastRatherThanPuttingHimDown` |
| **A man who is down takes no wound**; a blow that could have put him down or killed him standing finishes him (G0053, narrowing G0020). A stray pellet does not end a rescue; a barrel going off beside him, or claws, still do. | enforced - `VitalityTest.takesNoWoundWhileHeIsDown`, `.finishesAManWhoIsDownWithABlowThatWouldHavePutHimDownStanding` |
| Nothing on the alien side can be a casualty. Being rescuable requires somebody on your side who would come. | enforced - `CasualtyTest.killsAnAlienOutrightHoweverItWasHurt` |
| **The commander is one of his men in this too**: he is wounded, put down and killed by the same three harms, through the same table (G0023, G0053). | enforced - `VitalityTest.woundsTheCommanderByTheSameTableAsHisMen`, `CasualtyTest.leavesTheCommanderShotDownOnTheGroundRatherThanDead`, `.killsTheCommanderOutrightWhenTheBlowIsOneThatKills` |
| A man is put back on his feet by one path, whoever reached him, and **comes up with every wound cleared** (G0053). How long it takes and how long he is protected are facts about him, not about his rescuer. | judgement - `Aid` is the single writer, used by `ActionSystem` and `AidSystem`; `ActionTest.clearsEveryWoundWhenHeIsPutBackOnHisFeet` holds the half that is a rule |
| **How long he has on the ground is a fact about him too.** The commander's bleed-out is his own number and shorter than the squad's: what a player sits out is the bleed-out plus the throes plus the respawn, and three windows sized for a trooper added up to most of a fight. Shortening the shared one would have shortened every rescue on the field, which is a different decision about a different mechanic. | enforced - `CasualtyTest.countsTheCommanderDownOnHisOwnClockRatherThanHisMens`; one reader - `Spawner.spawnCommander` |
| A trooper will leave the level's objectives to fetch somebody, will not leave a standing order to, and will never leave a fight. | enforced - `AidTest.holdsWhereItWasToldToRatherThanGoingToFetchSomebody`, `.neverBreaksOffAFightToFetchSomebody`, `.putsAFallenComradeBackOnHisFeet` |
| Whether a trooper goes at all is a roll, taken once on the way in and never re-taken while it is on its way. Nobody is owed a rescue. | enforced - `AidTest.leavesHimWhereHeLiesWhenNobodyIsWilling`, `.walksAcrossToAManWhoIsDownOutOfReach` |
| A casualty is out of the fight entirely - not driven, not shooting, not holding ground - and is visibly distinct from a corpse. | enforced - `CasualtyTest.leavesACasualtyOutOfTheFight`, `.showsACasualtyAsOneAndCountsHisBleedOutDownWhereThePlayerCanSeeIt` |
| A bleed-out nobody answered leaves exactly the corpse a bullet would have. There is one death path. | enforced - `CasualtyTest.killsACasualtyNobodyReachedInTime`; `Deaths.kill` is the single writer |
| Stabilising is a commitment, not a total: letting go loses the work rather than banking it. | enforced - `ActionTest.losesTheWorkDoneSoFarWhenHeLetsGo` |
| Work being done on a man is drawn in a colour of its own; every other thing that fills up is the body's colour brightened. Two circles that ask for opposite reactions must not read alike. | enforced - `ActionTest.drawsTheWorkInItsOwnColourAndTheBleedOutInTheOrdinaryOne`, `.stopsColouringHimGreenTheMomentHeLetsGo` |
| Only something with nothing else driving it can be hauled - a man who is down, a barrel, or a turret nobody is on. A trooper on his feet cannot be picked up, and neither can a gun somebody is firing. | enforced - `DragTest.willNotTakeHoldOfAManStillOnHisFeet`, `.haulsABarrelAsReadilyAsAMan`, `TurretTest.willNotTakeHoldOfATurretSomebodyIsOnIt`, `.haulsAnEmptyTurretAsReadilyAsABarrel` |
| A drag names its load by an issued id, never by an entity reference (G0010, G0016). | judgement - `Drag.load` and `Dragged.by`; a recycled component resets to zero, so a stale id can never match |
| Hauling costs a hand: no firing, no sweep, no throw while something is in them. | enforced - `DragTest.leavesHimNoHandForTheGun` |
| A man hauling something can only back away from it, with a little sideways lean, and slower than he walks. | enforced - `DragTest.barelyMovesAtAllWhenHePressesStraightAtTheManHeIsDragging`, `.haulsHimSlowerThanTheCommanderWalks` |
| A load is moved by writing its velocity, never its position, so terrain still has the last word about where it may go. | enforced - `DragTest.putsABarrelBackToSceneryWhenHeLetsGoOfIt` holds the half that can drift; `DragSystem` never writes a position |
| **A man is hurt in wounds, not health** (G0053). Every blow declares a harm - wound, down or kill - and nothing about a man's fate is derived from an amount; one wound past his maximum puts him down. | enforced - `VitalityTest.carriesNoHealthOnAManAtAll`, `.putsHimDownWithOneWoundMoreThanHeCanCarry`, `.putsHimDownWithADownBlowWhateverHisWounds`, `.killsHimOutrightWithAKillingBlow` |
| Several wounds landing on one tick are one wound, and the **grace** after a wound turns away another wound and nothing else. | enforced - `VitalityTest.countsSeveralWoundsLandingOnOneTickAsOne`, `.turnsAWoundAwayDuringGrace`, `.letsABlowThatPutsHimDownThroughGrace`, `.letsTheWorstHarmOfATickWin` |
| Everything that heals a man clears every wound - being revived, a medkit, mending - through one method on `Vitality`. | enforced - `ActionTest.clearsEveryWoundWhenHeIsPutBackOnHisFeet`, `MedkitTest.clearsEveryWoundOfAWoundedManWhoWalksOntoIt`, `UpgradeTest.mendsTheChoosersWoundsOnTheirOwnAndNobodyElses`; one writer - `Vitality.recover` |
| Wounds belong to a life: a commander who respawns has none (G0044). His maximum is a stat and belongs to his slot (G0049). | enforced - `RespawnSystemTest.bringsItBackWithNoWounds`; the maximum becomes a stat in H4 |
| A wounded man is drawn as one, on the field and on his panel, and the number of his wounds can be read off both (pillar 5). | enforced - `VitalityTest.showsHowManyWoundsHeIsCarryingWhereThePlayerCanSeeIt`, `ReadoutWriterTest.publishesTheWoundsHeIsCarryingAndHowManyHeCould` |
| A wound costs a man in one place each - his pace in `Pace`, his gun in `Guns.spend` - and never his hand strike or his grenade. | enforced - `WoundCostTest.slowsAWoundedMan`, `.makesAWoundedManWaitLongerBetweenShots`, `.leavesAWoundedMansHandStrikeAlone`; one reader each - `Pace.of`, `Guns.spend` |
| **A shield takes exactly one hit of any harm** - a grab and a stun included - and is gone; the shove still lands. A commander comes back behind one that also runs out, and is drawn running down. | enforced - `ShieldTest.takesOneHitOfAnyHarmInsteadOfHim`, `.letsTheNextHitThroughOnceItHasBroken`, `.neverStacks`, `.turnsAStunAsReadilyAsAWound`, `.stillLetsTheShoveOfTheHitItTookLand`, `.showsHowMuchOfItIsLeftWhereThePlayerCanSeeIt`, `AntTest.takesNobodyWhoHasAShieldInFrontOfHim`, `RespawnSystemTest.bringsItBackBehindAShield`, `.letsTheShieldHeCameBackBehindRunOut`; it breaks in two places - `DamageSystem` for a hit, `CollisionSystem` for a grab |
| A stun a source declares is the ordinary `Stunned`, lasts what the source says scaled by one stat, and a stunned man is drawn distinctly from a staggered one. | enforced - `StunTest.stunsAManAShotFromAStunningGunHits`, `.letsHimGoOnceTheStunHasRunOut`, `.drawsAStaggerAsSomethingElse`; one reader - `Modifiers.stunTicks` (`STUN_DURATION`) |
| **Thick skin and self-treatment move a man's numbers through the one read each** - his maximum wounds where a hit is weighed, folded every tick so what he is drawn with is what the next hit meets; his mend where the bleed-out clock runs, from his *last* wound (G0053, G0049). | enforced - `UpgradeTest.carriesAnotherWoundOnHisFeetWithThickSkin`, `.mendsTheChoosersWoundsOnTheirOwnAndNobodyElses`, `.startsTheMendAgainWithEveryNewWound`, `.mendsNothingWithoutIt`; one reader each - `Modifiers.maxWounds` from `DamageSystem`, `Modifiers.mendSeconds` from `CasualtySystem` |
