# Invariants: Players, commanders and input

Part of the invariants indexed in [`../README.md`](../README.md#invariants), which says what a
status means. If a change would break a row here, stop and raise it rather than deciding alone.

| Invariant | Status |
|---|---|
| Input reaches the simulation as a per-tick value object; no system polls a device. | enforced - `ArchitectureTest.simulationDoesNotReadInputDirectly` |
| **A slot drives one commander and nobody else's.** One input per player per tick, handed out by `Simulation.step` before any system runs. | enforced - `CoopTest.drivesEachCommanderFromItsOwnSlot`, `.leavesASlotNobodyIsSittingInIdle` |
| A stick sends its aim as an **offset from the commander**, because it does not know where he is standing; exactly one place turns that into a point on the field, and no system below it sees two kinds of aim. | enforced - `CoopTest.aimsRelativeToWhicheverCommanderIsAiming`; one writer - `Simulation.step` |
| **No commander may walk further than the leash from the middle of the commanders at his machine** - players sharing a screen share a camera that must frame them; a player at a machine of his own has a camera of his own and no rope to anybody else (G0056). It refuses the outward part of a step and **never pushes**, so nobody is shoved and a man at the end of the rope walks home himself. A backstop against two players on one screen ending up a level apart, deliberately far wider than the view - **so it does not hold pillar 4 on its own, and a player may be off screen.** | enforced - `CoopTest.refusesToLetTwoCommandersWalkFurtherApartThanTheLeash`, `.letsHimWalkBackTowardsTheGroupFromTheEndOfTheLeash`, `.leavesASoleCommanderFreeToWalkWhereverHeLikes`, `.letsTwoPlayersAtDifferentMachinesWalkAsFarApartAsTheyLike`, `.holdsTwoPlayersAtOneMachineTogetherWhileAThirdPlaysFromAnother`; online `SessionTest.seatsEachPlayerAtTheMachineHePlaysFrom` |
| The leash is a **distance in world units**, never read from the camera. Zoom is a rendering setting and must not become extra rope. | judgement - `sim.commander.leashRadius`; the row above holds the half that is testable |
| A commander comes back **at the rally point** - the spawn area until a point is taken, the point taken last after that - even with another player still on his feet (G0055). One place for every man the reserve sends, and it is ground the side holds. | enforced - `CoopTest.bringsHimBackAtTheRallyPointEvenWithAFriendStillOnHisFeet`, `RespawnSystemTest.bringsItBackInTheSpawnAreaWhileNoPointIsTaken`, `ReserveTest.bringsACommanderBackAtTheRallyPointRatherThanWhereHeFell` |
| A commander comes back **into the slot he left**, so a death does not reseat a player. | enforced - `CoopTest.bringsHimBackIntoTheSlotHeLeft` |
| **A commander can redeploy only after `redeployAfterSeconds` of his bleed-out has run** (G0109) - counted from what has run, so a stopped clock holds it shut - and only on a press: a key held from before does nothing. Redeploying is the ordinary death, at the reserve's cost; a trooper never redeploys, and nobody redeploys another. | enforced - `RedeployTest.doesNothingOnAPressBeforeHisWindowIsOpen`, `.doesNothingForAKeyHeldFromBeforeHisWindowOpened`, `.endsHisBleedOutOnAPressOnceHisWindowIsOpen`, `.bringsHimBackAfterTheUsualThroesAndDelayAtTheReservesCost`, `.opensHisWindowOnlyOnceThatMuchOfHisBleedOutHasRun`, `.neverOpensATroopersWindow` |
| **A commander who is down wears the down mark for every player on every machine** (G0109) - state, never a prompt, so no machine's PROMPTS switch hides it; a trooper down does not. | enforced - `RedeployTest.marksACommanderWhoIsDownAndSaysWhenHisWindowOpens`; how it looks is judgement |
| Every player's commander is drawn in a colour of his own. Four identical circles is not a field anybody can read (pillar 5). | enforced - `GraphicConfigTest.givesEveryPlayerACommanderColourOfHisOwn` |
| Aim is independent of movement; no system assumes facing follows heading (G0002). | enforced - `CommanderControlSystemTest.firesWhereItAimsNoMatterWhereItIsWalking` |
| The commander is an ordinary entity - killable, collidable, standing on the field. | enforced - `CombatTest.killsTheCommanderTheMomentAnEnemyTouchesIt` |
| The commander comes back while any trooper still lives: with the reserve empty, a trooper on his feet is **recalled** to pay for him (G0121) - the one nearest the rally point, never a man down, ties by handle, one per waiting commander lowest slot first - and it is not a death: no throes, no blast, no corpse, his own fade. | enforced - `ReserveTest.recallsATrooperToBringACommanderBackOnAnEmptyReserve`, `.paysFromTheReserveBeforeRecallingAnybody`, `.recallsTheTrooperNearestTheRallyPoint`, `.neverRecallsAManWhoIsDown`, `.breaksATieByHandleOrder`, `.recallsOnePerWaitingCommanderLowestSlotFirst`, `.bringsEveryWaitingCommanderBackWhileThereAreTroopersToRecall`, `.recallsHimWithoutADeath`; `ReadoutWriterTest.saysHeWasPaidForByARecall` |
| **Aim is a direction, never a distance.** Zoom is a rendering setting and cannot become extra range. | enforced - `CommanderControlSystemTest.firesInTheSameDirectionHoweverFarAwayTheAimPointIs` |
| The commander's facing is his aim and never his heading (G0002). | enforced - `FacingTest.facesWhereTheCommanderIsAimingRatherThanWhereHeIsWalking` |
| The action key does whatever is within arm's length, nearest first; the player never selects (G0021). **Four things are now on that list** - a casualty, a gun, a crate, a shut door - so nearest is doing real work: a tie goes to the gun, because getting on one is the reversible half of the pair, then the crate, then the casualty, and a door last (G0069). What he takes from a crate is the one choice the key leads to, in the top bar (G0056). A door is opened on stabilise's rules: held, and lost to letting go, a step away, going down or a wound. **What it would act on is published by handle** (`Action.subject`, nobody when nothing is on offer) from that one choice, for the interface to mark - it never chooses again (G0098). | enforced - `ActionTest.offersToPickUpAManWhoIsDownBesideHim`, `.offersNothingForAManWhoIsDownOutOfReach`, `.stopsOfferingWhenHeWalksOutOfReach`, `TurretTest.offersTheTurretToAManStandingBesideIt`, `CrateTest.offersToOpenACrateWithinArmsLength`, `DoorTest.offersToOpenAShutDoorWithinArmsLength`, `.offersAManDownBesideHimBeforeADoorAsNear`, `.opensOnTheActionKeyHeldAtItForOpenSeconds`, `.losesTheWorkWhenHeLetsGo`, `.losesTheWorkWhenAWoundLandsOnHim`, `.losesTheWorkWhenHeGoesDown`, `ActionTest.namesTheManDownBesideHimAsTheSubject`, `.namesNobodyWhenThereIsNothingToDo`, `DoorTest.namesTheShutDoorAsTheSubject`, `.namesTheManDownBesideHimRatherThanADoorAsNear`, `TurretTest.namesTheTurretHeIsOnAsTheSubject`, `CrateTest.namesNobodyWhileHeHasTheCrateOpen`, `AidTest.namesTheManHeIsPickingUpAsTheSubject` |
| **A dash never protects him** (G0107, pillar 2): no invulnerability, no shield, no i-frames - a blow that lands mid-dash lands. It buys distance and nothing else. | enforced - `DashTest.neverProtectsHim` |
| **A dash goes along his movement, never his aim** (G0107, G0002): with the stick at rest, the way he last moved; his facing only if he never has.
