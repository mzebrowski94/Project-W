# G0001: Inspired by *Triumph! War 2099*, not a remake of it

**Trigger.** Proposing content from the source game, or naming a unit, weapon or level.

**Enforced by.** Nothing mechanical - caught by reading.

**Constraint.** We take the source game's feel - top-down, a commander leading a squad, arcade pace
with tactical decisions - and nothing else. Levels, unit roster, weapons and progression are ours.

**Scope.** Vocabulary is the exception: where the source has a good word for a thing, we use it, and
`docs/glossary.md` marks those *(source)*. Borrowing a term is not a commitment to borrowing the
mechanic behind it.

**DO NOT BUILD** - rejected, and likely to be re-proposed. A faithful remake - reconstructing the nine levels and
the original roster. It sounds like a way to skip design work, but the source is a 2001 Multimedia
Fusion game whose details are barely documented; reconstructing them accurately would cost more
than designing our own, and would bind us to decisions made for a four-player co-op game we are not
building.

**Revisit when** never, realistically. Deciding partway through to match the original would
invalidate the levels and roster already built.
